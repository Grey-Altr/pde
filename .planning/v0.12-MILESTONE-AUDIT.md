## Critical Findings

### P0 — OTR Glob Mismatch (Blocks Deploy)

**Producer:** `handoff.md` writes `OTR-outreach-sequences-v{N}.md`
**Consumer:** `deploy.md` line 117 globs `OTR-outreach-v*.md`
**Impact:** Deploy halts at preflight — Gates 3 and 4 unreachable
**Fix:** Change deploy.md glob to `OTR-outreach-sequences-v*.md`
**Affected:** KIT-03, DEPLOY-04, DEPLOY-06

### P1 — BTH Glob Mismatch (Corrupts LKT Manifest)

**Producer:** `brief.md` writes `BTH-thesis-v{N}.md`
**Consumers:** handoff.md:594, wireframe.md:624, critique.md:812 glob `BTH-business-thesis-v*.md`
**Impact:** BTH always reports missing; DPD defaults to yc_10 without funding signal
**Fix:** Change all 3 consumer globs to `BTH-thesis-v*.md`
**Affected:** BRIEF-03, KIT-01, LAUNCH-03, QUAL-01, QUAL-02

### P2 — hasDeployStaging Never Written

**Consumer:** `build.md` reads `hasDeployStaging` for Stage 14 completion
**Missing:** `deploy.md` never sets this flag in design-manifest.json
**Impact:** Stage 14 permanently shows "pending" on /pde:build re-run
**Fix:** Add `hasDeployStaging` as 21st designCoverage field, write after Gate 4 completes
**Affected:** DEPLOY-01, DEPLOY-09

### P3 — handoff.md Missing required_reading

**Missing:** business-track.md, launch-frameworks.md, business-financial-disclaimer.md, business-legal-disclaimer.md
**Impact:** Agent may not pre-load business context before reaching business-mode steps
**Fix:** Add 4 references to handoff.md required_reading block
**Affected:** KIT-01 through KIT-06
